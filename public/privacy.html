Privacy policy

OrbitPros AI Ops Setup helps a store owner connect their business tools to an AI assistant. This page says what the app stores and why.

What we store

  • Your store's myshopify.com address.
  • Your store's name, currency and number of products, read from Shopify to tailor the example questions the app suggests.
  • The choices you make in the app: which assistant you use, which tools you use, and which you have marked as connected.

What we do not store

  • No customer names, emails, addresses or orders.
  • No Shopify access tokens. The app requests a short-lived token when you open it and discards it after reading the details above.
  • No data from the tools you connect. Each connector signs you in directly with that service, and this app is never in between.

Sharing

We do not sell or share the stored information with anyone.

Deletion

Everything stored for your store is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also ask us to delete it at any time.
